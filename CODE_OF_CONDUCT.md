# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Being respectful of differing opinions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or derogatory comments.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Inappropriate behavior in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate corrective action.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual represents the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when determining consequences:

1. Correction: A private warning.
2. Warning: A warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. Temporary Ban: A temporary restriction from community participation.
4. Permanent Ban: A permanent restriction from community participation.
